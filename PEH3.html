Section 1: Health-Related Fitness Components

  • 1. Cardiorespiratory Endurance — The ability of the heart, lungs, and blood vessels to deliver oxygen during sustained physical activity.
  • 2. Muscular Strength — The maximum amount of force a muscle or muscle group can exert in a single effort.
  • 3. Muscular Endurance — The ability of a muscle to sustain repeated contractions over a period of time.
  • 4. Flexibility — The range of motion available at a joint; important for graceful dance movements.
  • 5. Body Composition — The relative percentage of lean mass to fat mass in the body.

Section 2: Common Folk Dance Terms

  • 1. Saludo — A bow or greeting gesture performed at the beginning or end of a dance.
  • 2. Hayon-Hayon — A gesture where one hand is placed at the waist while the other hand moves in a waving motion, often alternating.
  • 3. Sarok — A gesture where one arm crosses the body, typically with the hand reaching toward the opposite hip or shoulder.
  • 4. Do-Si-Do — A movement where two dancers pass each other shoulder to shoulder, then step back to back before returning to face each other.
  • 5. Kumintang — A circular motion of the hand and wrist, often used to accentuate arm movements in Philippine folk dances.
  • 6. Cabesang — Refers to movements led by the head or a bowing gesture; often performed with the hand holding the head or gesturing near the forehead.
  • 7. Crossed Arms — Both arms folded across the chest, commonly seen in various Philippine folk dance formations.

Arm Position 1

Both arms are curved in front of the chest, fingertips almost touching, forming a circle or oval shape. Elbows are relaxed and slightly away from the body.

Arm Position 2

Both arms are extended sideward at shoulder level, slightly curved downward. Palms face down with fingers together and thumbs tucked in.

Arm Position 3

One arm is raised overhead in a curved position; the other remains extended sideward at shoulder level. The raised arm creates a graceful arch above the head.

Arm Position 4

One arm is raised overhead in a curve; the other is extended forward at shoulder level, palm down. Both arms maintain a soft, rounded line.

Arm Position 5

Both arms are raised overhead, fingertips almost touching, forming a complete circle or oval above the head. Elbows are gently curved outward.

Feet Position 1

Heels are placed together and toes are turned out apart (approximately 45 degrees). Legs are straight and touching each other.

Feet Position 2

Feet are placed apart sideward at shoulder distance, toes pointing forward. Weight is evenly distributed on both feet.

Feet Position 3

The heel of one foot is placed in front of the instep (arch) of the other foot. The feet form a right angle shape with slight separation.

Feet Position 4

One foot is placed in front of the other at a comfortable distance. Both toes point forward, with the back foot slightly turned out.

Feet Position 5

The heel of one foot is placed directly in front of and touching the toes of the other foot. Both feet form a straight line.

Section 4: Common Folk Dance Steps

Polka Series 2/4 Time

  • Plain Polka — Step, close step (pause). Count: Ah, 1 & 2 &
  • Hop Polka — Hop step, close step (pause). Count: 1, 2, 1, 2 &
  • Heel and Toe Polka — Heel place, Toe place, Step, close step (pause). Count: 1, 2, 1, 2 &
  • Slide Polka — Slide, close step (pause). Count: 1, 2, 1, 2 &

Waltz Series 3/4 Time

  • Native Waltz — Step, close, step. Count: 1, 2, 3
  • Waltz Balance — Step, close, raise heels up, down. Count: 1, 2, 3
  • Cross Waltz — Cross-step, close, step. Count: 1, 2, 3

Sway Balance Series 3/4 Time

  • With a point — Step, cross-step/Step, point.
  • With a waltz — Step, cross-step/Step, close.
  • With a raise — Step, cross-step/Step, raise.
  • With a brush — Step, cross-step/Step, brush.
  • Double sway balance — Step, cross-step/Step, close.

Other Steps

  • Bacui 3/4 Time — Cross-point step, cross-step rear, cross-step front, close (pause).
  • Bicking 2/4 or 3/4 Time — Heel-place, close.
  • Change Step 2/4 Time — Step, close, step.
  • Cross Change Step 2/4 Time — Cross-step, step.

Advanced Folk Dance Steps

  • Contra-ganza 2/4 Time — Leap sideward, cross-step, step.
  • Haplik — Step, step, hop, hop, three-step turn, hop, hop, repeat.
  • Mazurka 3/4 Time — Slide, cut, hop.
  • Jotudansa 3/4 Time — Step, heel brush, heel-step (3x right, 3x left), step-close-step with turn.
  • Redoba 3/4 Time — Slide, cut, cut.
  • Engano Series 3/4 Time — Variations with waltz, close, heel-place.
  • Paso Español 3/4 Time — Step, close heels up/down, brush-swing.
  • Papuri 3/4 Time — Step, cross-step, cross-point.
  • Sangi 3/4 Time — Step, close, circle in air.
  • Escotis 2/4 Time — Step, close, step, hop.
